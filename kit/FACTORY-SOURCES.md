# Factory composition provenance

Maintainer: harness-kit maintainers. This is inferred composition guidance under
[spec 15, choices 17/34/38/41 and A27/A36](https://github.com/hanh9898/harness-kit/issues/15)
and [ticket 30](https://github.com/hanh9898/harness-kit/issues/30), not a new runtime,
consumer deployment or proof of unattended operation.

The [accepted task/flow source map](team-workflow/SOURCES.md) owns the original
task-contract, work-item, ten flow functions, conditional claim/off-switch/loop
requirements and selected brake/andon/adoption provenance. Its primary-source
corroboration retains its original date, adaptation and unavailable historical
source limits. Factory composition reuses those meanings through links, rather
than duplicating task or policy definitions. [Authority sources](authority/SOURCES.md)
own function ceilings and their separation from stage and grants.

The owner explicitly selected Matt Pocock's original `software-factory` 1.3.2
for this implementation. Its actual SKILL.md and EXAMPLE-TASKS.md were read.
They determine the service/task distinction, complete service slots, task/source
questions, restrained triggers with brakes, bounded review, and ending runs for
human re-entry. The original provider retains the method body and invocation
contract; this kit does not package a copy or unlock its explicit-only setting.
A consuming request must resolve the selected original through its own permitted
provider route. Surveys apply when product selection is actually requested;
this change selects no product for a consumer and makes no current vendor claim.

Existing #25 authority, #26 tasks/team, #27 operations, #28 observation/assessment
and #29 recovery retain ownership of their contracts. N06 determines focused
pointers; E07/E08 inform current state and logical history without requiring a
new logging implementation. The new contribution is the connected arrangement
and per-path readiness assessment through the existing entries. Qualification
conditions, original-method digests and observed effects are retained with the
implementation evidence, separately from source-backed design claims.
