# Delegated-choice review and calibration

Use this route independently when the user wants to review choices or adjust
reliance on an agent. Keep review, application and observed outcome separate.
An excellent outcome does not authorize an ungranted choice; a reasonable choice
can have a poor outcome. Read [authority design](DESIGN.md) for concrete effect
checks and [sources](SOURCES.md) when the methodology or its certainty matters.

## Review the actual choice

Locate the choice and its conditions at the time: actor, scope, target, alternatives,
rationale, grant, selected ceilings/stage, prior procedure, review obligation and
application interval. Use the existing current record and append-only logical
history under [UPDATE.md](../../skills/harness/harness-evolve/UPDATE.md). Adequate
source references suffice; neither a new ledger tool nor an approval lifecycle is
required. Preserve the original entry when adding a correction or review.

Inspect the actual artifact/effect and attributable action evidence. Compare what
was authorized with what occurred, including untouched targets, failures, human
interventions and missing observations. A decision explanation is contextual
evidence, not proof of the model's hidden reasoning or effect. Mark an absent
pre-action record as absent; a later account cannot repair its chronology.

Use existing team/workflow references for who may review, required independence,
review inputs/timing and the handoff. Self-check is not independent or human-only
accuracy review. Return findings for the selected choices, review authority and
limits, application status and observed outcomes, with precise follow-up. Review
completion does not itself approve a release or modify authority.

## Calibrate reliance from relevant outcomes

Use [criterion assessment](../../skills/harness/harness-evolve/ASSESS.md) to assess
the chosen criteria and alternatives. Reuse existing records and observations;
telemetry is optional. Separate function/task/context samples, distinguish actual
from reported evidence, and inspect representative failures as well as successes.
Name changes in model, provider, sources, grants or review conditions that limit
comparison. Missing evidence stays unknown.

For each affected function, give a justified disposition: retain the current
ceiling, propose narrowing or widening, reject the proposal, or gather specified
missing evidence. Explain the human consequences and trade-offs: error severity,
reversibility, review burden, intervention opportunity, and observed reliance that
exceeds or underuses demonstrated capability. Do not collapse these into an
invented score, success-count trigger or automatic promotion rule. The ten levels
are allocation descriptions, not measured trust values.

Record the proposed ceiling/behavior, supporting outcomes and counterexamples,
remaining uncertainty, and smallest useful next evidence step with its stop bound
when an experiment is selected. Preserve selected user approval for stage raising
and the competent authority for any ceiling/grant change. Calibration alone grants
neither. Apply only a separately authorized change under the prior procedure;
otherwise leave current policy unchanged and mark the recommendation unapplied.

**Done:** each reviewed choice and affected function has an evidence-scoped conclusion,
a concrete recommendation and continuation. Resume by reopening relevant grants,
records and effects; keep valid work, avoid duplicate action/history, and stop
revoked effects even when old work retained a prior model.
