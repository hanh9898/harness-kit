# Criterion assessment

Assess the selected outcome independently of observation, experimentation or adoption. Reuse supplied artifacts, human review, checks and existing operating evidence. Telemetry is optional; qualitative evidence can answer a qualitative criterion. When operating facts need correlation, use [OBSERVE.md](OBSERVE.md). An unavailable source limits its dependent conclusion, not every criterion.

Read kit `H15` for stated/inferred/open evidence and `H02` for representative cases and a criterion-specific rubric through the entry's kit CLI. Their guidance informs the assessment; selecting H02 does not require installing an evaluation platform. Read `I06` only for a measured-improvement experiment: its noise and held-out-case guidance does not impose a composite score or an experiment on direct assessment. Select original methods through [CHANGE-FLOW.md](CHANGE-FLOW.md) for the actual work. Supplied-fact assessment alone is not guidance authoring; an authorized agent-read assessment record does require the selected writing discipline.

For whether an action is permitted, delegated-choice review or authority calibration,
read [authority guidance](../../../kit/authority/DESIGN.md) before the judgment.
It supplies the authority model and concrete effect check; this assessment supplies
evidence interpretation. A grant question can finish without redesign or adoption.

## Establish the comparison

Use the user's selected criteria, thresholds and applicable standards. Identify the assessed artifact/version, consuming target, sample and time, evidence sources, comparison point and material conditions. Reuse settled criteria; ask only when a missing consequential criterion prevents a useful conclusion. Label proposed criteria as proposals. Distinguish selected requirements, checks performed and requirements actually satisfied.

Separate current quality from improvement. Without a suitable baseline, assess the current artifact and mark change unknown. A retrospective baseline needs attributable artifacts and comparable conditions; a remembered impression is not a fabricated measurement. With a baseline, explain comparability, sample limits, variability and confounding changes. Source-reported tests remain source-reported unless actually rerun. Do not claim that a passing check, application or one promising sample establishes general improvement.

## Give a justified disposition for every selected criterion

For each criterion provide the evidence and scope, result (including failure, regression or uncertainty), recommendation and reason, trade-off, and smallest useful next step:

| Recommendation | Use and completion |
| --- | --- |
| Retain | Evidence supports keeping this behavior within stated conditions; name residual risks and what would change the conclusion. |
| Revise | A specific shortfall or regression warrants a bounded proposed change; preserve useful parts and name the verification needed. |
| Gather | The conclusion needs a particular missing source, baseline or comparison; name the minimum evidence and its prerequisite, then stop rather than loop. |
| Reject | Evidence or a binding constraint rules out the candidate in this scope; explain why and preserve a valid prior choice where possible. |

Mixed recommendations can address different parts of one criterion; make those parts explicit. A passing criterion can coexist with a regression elsewhere. Report quality, actual/estimated cost and elapsed work separately with units and coverage. Preserve trade-offs and any binding veto; invent no aggregate score or unstated weighting to erase them. Unknown evidence stays unknown rather than passing, failing or becoming zero by default. A recommendation to retain is not adoption, and rejection can complete a requested learning result.

**Done:** every selected criterion has a supported or explicitly limited conclusion and an actionable recommendation. The user can see what the evidence decides, what it cannot decide and the consequences of the alternatives.

## Close or continue within the request

For assessment only, return conclusions and continuation without edits or activation. Keep selection, decision review, application and observed outcome distinct. Further experiments, telemetry, repair and adoption require their own applicable scope and grants; a useful next-step suggestion is not authorization to perform it.

For an authorized save, use [UPDATE.md](UPDATE.md) to preserve the assessment's objective, criteria, versions, source references, original conditions, recommendations, unknowns and next trigger in the existing discoverable record. Preserve prior choice history and mark recommendations as unapplied. On resume, inspect the record and changing live sources first, retain completed work, and update only conclusions affected by new evidence. Required unavailable methods or failed durable capture block the dependent write with an exact prerequisite and continuation; independent assessment can still finish in the conversation.
